Uvod i Prikupljanje Podataka Introduction & Data Collection

KortikBot je napredni Discord bot dizajniran za sigurnost, muziku i moderaciju. Naš prioritet je vaša privatnost. Za razliku od mnogih drugih botova, KortikBot radi na principu obrade u realnom vremenu (RAM memorija) i ne čuva trajno vaše poruke na disku. KortikBot is an advanced Discord bot designed for security, music, and moderation. Our priority is your privacy. Unlike many other bots, KortikBot operates on a real-time processing principle (RAM memory) and does not permanently store your messages on disk.

Koje podatke obrađujemo? What data do we process?

  • ID Korisnika: (Discord User ID) - koristi se isključivo za izvršavanje komandi i logove. User ID: (Discord User ID) - used exclusively for command execution and logs.
  • Sadržaj Poruka: Privremeno se skenira radi moderacije (anti-spam, psovke, scam linkovi) i odmah briše iz memorije. Message Content: Temporarily scanned for moderation (anti-spam, profanity, scam links) and immediately deleted from memory.
  • Audio Upiti: Nazivi pesama ili linkovi koje unosite za puštanje muzike. Ne snimamo vaš glas. Audio Queries: Song titles or links you enter to play music. We do not record your voice.
  • Logovi Moderacije: Informacije o kaznama (ban, kick, mute) se čuvaju radi funkcionisanja servera. Moderation Logs: Information about punishments (ban, kick, mute) is stored for server functionality.

Gde idu vaši podaci? Where does your data go?

Nigde. Vaši podaci se NE dele sa trećim stranama, oglašivačima niti drugim kompanijama. Pristup logovima imaju isključivo: Nowhere. Your data is NOT shared with third parties, advertisers, or other companies. Access to logs is granted exclusively to:

  • Vlasnik Discord servera na kojem se bot nalazi. The owner of the Discord server where the bot resides.
  • Administratori tog servera (prema dozvolama koje im je vlasnik dao). Administrators of that server (according to permissions granted by the owner).
  • Glavni developer bota (samo u svrhu debagovanja kritičnih grešaka). The main bot developer (only for debugging critical errors).

Sigurnost Security

Svi podaci se procesuiraju putem enkriptovane konekcije sa Discord API-jem. Bot koristi napredne algoritme za zaštitu od zloupotrebe. All data is processed via an encrypted connection with the Discord API. The bot uses advanced algorithms to protect against abuse.
